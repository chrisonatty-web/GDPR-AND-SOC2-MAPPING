## Project Overview

The NEXUS GDPR and SOC 2 Mapping Project demonstrates how a technology company can develop a unified Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) approach to address multiple regulatory and security frameworks without creating disconnected or duplicated compliance programmes.

Nexus aims to expand into the European market while demonstrating its ability to meet GDPR obligations and preparing for SOC 2. To support these objectives, this project focuses on identifying compliance gaps, mapping common controls, assigning control owners, collecting evidence and establishing continuous monitoring and improvement.

The project demonstrates how a coordinated multi-framework compliance strategy can strengthen an organisation's control environment, improve accountability and support business expansion.

## Business Problem Statement

Nexus faces a significant compliance challenge as it prepares to expand into the European market.

The organisation needs to address GDPR requirements while preparing for SOC 2. However, implementing separate compliance programmes for each framework could create:

- Duplicated compliance activities.
- Fragmented risk management processes.
- Inefficient control implementation.
- Disconnected evidence collection.
- Unclear control ownership and accountability.
- Additional operational complexity.

The business requires a unified compliance approach that identifies overlapping requirements and establishes common controls to support multiple frameworks.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

1. Assess Nexus's SOC 2 readiness and identify control gaps.
2. Map relevant GDPR and SOC 2 requirements to common business processes and controls.
3. Identify weaknesses in the existing control environment.
4. Recommend solutions to address identified control gaps.
5. Assign control owners to establish accountability.
6. Identify and collect evidence demonstrating control implementation.
7. Establish monitoring and continuous improvement activities.
8. Develop a roadmap for strengthening the organisation's compliance posture.

## Key Features

### 1. Multi-Framework Compliance Mapping

Identifies common requirements across GDPR and SOC 2 to support a unified compliance programme rather than separate framework-specific initiatives.

### 2. SOC 2 Readiness Assessment

Conducts a readiness assessment to identify control gaps and weaknesses requiring remediation before SOC 2 compliance activities progress.

### 3. Common Control Identification

Maps requirements to common organisational controls, helping reduce duplicated compliance activities.

### 4. Risk and Control Ownership

Assigns responsibility for implementing and managing controls to appropriate business functions.

### 5. Evidence Collection

Identifies the evidence needed to demonstrate control implementation and support compliance monitoring.

### 6. Continuous Monitoring and Improvement

Establishes a monitoring approach to track control effectiveness, changes in risk and corrective actions.
